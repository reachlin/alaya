Fig. 1

The four layers 八識不是八個心

The doctrine insists the eight are functions of one stream, not eight minds in committee. So they share one store and one moment; they are not services. The senses present, the sixth deliberates and acts, the seventh colours, the eighth keeps — and only the sixth touches the world.

Why the seventh exists at all. The sixth consciousness's self-grasping is intermittent — you can argue yourself out of it, and it stops when you stop thinking. The dense, unbroken sense of being someone persists through periods of no deliberation, so something must be doing it constantly. Read as engineering: the self-model an agent carries is structurally a bias source, not a neutral fact. So it is written into every prompt and measured.
Fig. 2

One moment 三法展轉，因果同時

Seed, manifestation and newly-perfumed seed are not three steps. They are simultaneous — the circle has no starting point, which is why the doctrine needs neither a first cause nor an external world as a power source. In code that is a transaction: everything in a moment commits together or the moment did not happen.

Fig. 3

Two axes, not one 三性 × 三量

An early draft of this system tagged every claim on one axis and got it wrong. 三性 is an ontology — what kind of thing this is. 三量 is an epistemology — how it came to be known. They are orthogonal, and the doctrine keeps them apart: a rope really is there (依他起) whether or not the cognition of it is sound, and taking it for a snake is 非量, an error of knowing rather than of being.

Expect most claims to land in “overlaid.” That is not the gate malfunctioning. 遍計所執 is the ordinary condition of unawakened cognition — the doctrine's claim is that superimposition is what ordinary knowing consists of, not an occasional lapse. A gate reporting clean most of the time would be the broken one.
Fig. 4

The turning 六七因中轉，五八果上圓

Each consciousness becomes a wisdom — and they do not turn together. The sixth and seventh begin at 見道, while practice is still under way, because discrimination and self-grasping are what wisdom can reach directly. The senses and the store are perfected only at the fruit: they cannot be corrected from inside their own operation. That timing rule is the correct engineering split, and it is enforced rather than described.

Sched. A

Six invariants 種子六義

護法's six criteria for a valid seed are not commentary. Each closes a hole, and each is a property of the store that can fail — so each is a test.

義The criterionThe invariant in code
剎那滅arises and ceases with no abiding stageSeeds frozen and content-addressed. The store has no update and no delete — the operations are not defined.
果俱有seed, manifestation and fruit are simultaneousParents must have arisen in the same tick. A cause on record but absent raises. This is also what makes provenance total.
恆隨轉unbroken succession, 如瀑流Nothing removed; strength decays toward a floor above zero. A forgotten memory is a quiet one, not an absent one.
性決定善種生善現行 — moral category fixedValence set at write and hashed into the id; a derived seed cannot invert its parents'.
待眾緣manifests only when conditions are completeA seed fires only when all its conditions are present. Being in the store is not being active.
引自果色心不相互生 — categories do not crossA lineage continues as its own kind; a derived seed is never drawn from a mixture.
剎那滅 against 恆隨轉. A seed must perish every moment and persist until countered — which reads as a specification bug and is the most interesting thing in the design. The doctrine resolves it with 自類相續: the seed does not endure and change, it perishes and re-arises as one of its own kind. Taken literally: weight is never mutated. Reinforcement appends a new arising on the same lineage, and present strength is computed across the chain — so “why is this memory strong?” stays answerable.
Note B

Why it is not solipsism 共業

If all is consciousness-only, why do two agents see one kitchen? 《唯識二十論》 treats this as among the hardest objections and does not answer by conceding an external kitchen. It answers with 共業 — the receptacle world is 共變, manifested congruently by beings whose karma is shared.

So there is no shared store, and there must not be: the ālaya is individual, and a universal mind belongs to a different doctrine. Agents keep separate stores and exchange 共相種子 — content-addressed records each perfumes into its own stream and manifests for itself. The worlds look alike because the seeds are alike. That distinction is the whole doctrine, and it happens to be the better distributed design: no shared mutable state, only propagation of immutable records.
